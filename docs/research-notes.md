## Safe factual claims to use in site copy

The site may say that SOAR stands for Student Organized Academic Refinement and is a student-led organization centered on organized peer-led study sessions and peer-to-peer academic support, citing LinkedIn and the two external articles. It may say the organization’s public coverage describes its beginnings during the 2020 shift to virtual learning, use of online study resources and chat rooms, and support for reviewing course material rather than replacing classroom instruction, citing Panther Prints. It may say NBC DFW covered student-led study sessions in Plano ISD and the growth of the model to more student leaders and schools, citing NBC DFW.

The exact current impact metrics—7,500+ students reached, 100+ tutoring sessions, 50+ study guides, and 40+ subjects—are organization-provided in the supplied PDF and should be labeled as organization-provided/current metrics rather than independently verified. The supplied leadership names and roles are likewise organization-provided. School names and the “Supporting Students Across Plano ISD” statement are organization-provided and must include the clarification that they do not imply formal district endorsement or direct affiliation unless separately verified.
