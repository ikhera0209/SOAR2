## Design and copy direction
- Palette: deep navy, vibrant sky blue, yellow/golden accents matching the supplied logo.
- Tone: friendly, modern, academic—not corporate or overly childish.
- Generous whitespace; rounded cards; subtle gradients; restrained motion; accessible contrast; visible focus states; keyboard-friendly navigation; responsive layout.
- Use a professional serif or strong display font for headings with a highly legible sans-serif for body copy.
- Copy should be clear, optimistic, student-centered, and specific. Avoid “revolutionary,” “best,” “world-class,” “guaranteed results,” or claims that SOAR replaces teachers. Present SOAR as a peer-led supplement to classroom learning.
- Use “Student Organized Academic Refinement” prominently at least once.
